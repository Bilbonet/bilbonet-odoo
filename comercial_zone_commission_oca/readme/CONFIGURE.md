To configure commercial zone commissions:

- Go to *Commissions > Configuration > Commercial Zones*.
- Create a commercial zone.
- Add the agents responsible for that zone in the *Zone Agents* tab.
- Assign commercial zones to the relevant states or provinces.
- Review or manually adjust the commercial zone on customer contact forms when
  needed.

Zone agents must be valid commission agents.

Commercial zones can only be created from the commercial zone maintenance
menu. Many2one fields that select commercial zones do not allow creating new
zones directly.
