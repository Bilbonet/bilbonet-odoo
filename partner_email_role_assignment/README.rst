Usage
=====

This module enhances Partner management by allowing users to define
specific email purposes. When creating or editing a contact in a Partner
form, two checkboxes let you mark the email as used for **sales** or
**invoicing**.

|Multi Mail Contact Fomr|

Marked contacts are automatically listed under the **Email Addresses**
tab in their respective sections and are included by default when
sending sales or invoice-related emails.
