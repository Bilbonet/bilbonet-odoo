This module adds partner delivery zone in different parts of the picking
process (views, filters and even logic).

It's useful when you delivery to customers with your own resources and
delivery zones.

Use Cases / Context
===================

The company handles freight delivery to customers using our own
resources. We have our own carriers to deliver products to various
zones.

To organize shipments, we use batch picking and collaborate with partner
delivery zones
